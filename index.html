Ground Control — Operations Manual

Manage arriving and departing aircraft, coordinate runway movements, guide traffic to gates, and dispatch ground-service vehicles.

Arrival operations

  1. Inbound aircraft appear outside the airport.
  2. Select an inbound aircraft from Active Traffic.
  3. Use Vector to Final to guide it onto final approach.
  4. Once established on final, issue landing clearance.
  5. After landing, use Taxi to Gate.
  6. The aircraft follows its taxi route and parks.

Departure operations

  1. Create a departure and select it.
  2. Choose its runway and departure route.
  3. Disconnect the jetway and finish ground services.
  4. Request pushback.
  5. Taxi to the holding point.
  6. Cross runways when cleared.
  7. Line up and issue takeoff clearance.

Runway directions

Runway numbers represent magnetic headings rounded to the nearest ten degrees.

  • 09: eastbound.
  • 27: westbound.
  • 08: eastbound.
  • 26: westbound.
  • 17: southbound.
  • 35: northbound.

Ground services

  • Jetway: connect or disconnect the passenger bridge.
  • Fuel: dispatch a fuel vehicle.
  • Catering: dispatch a catering truck.
  • Baggage: dispatch a baggage vehicle.
  • Pushback: move an aircraft away from its gate.

Waypoints

Waypoints mark taxiway intersections, holding points, and apron areas. Select a waypoint and route an aircraft to it.

Traffic management

Select aircraft using the traffic list or by clicking them on the map. Hold Position stops movement. Resume restarts a route. Cancel Route stops the current route.

This is a simplified airport operations game, not a certified ATC training system.
